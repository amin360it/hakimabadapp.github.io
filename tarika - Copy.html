About This Lineage

A school of mystical thought propounded by Hazrat Mojaddede Alfe Sani (rh.). This spiritual lineage traces back through 35+ saints from Prophet Muhammad (SAW) to the present day, representing the chain of spiritual transmission (ijazah) that continues to guide seekers on the path to Allah.

The tradition combines the teachings of multiple Sufi orders: Qadria, Chistia, Naqshbandia, and Mujaddedia.
