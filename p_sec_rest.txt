<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Having set aright the basic articles of faith, it is necessary to occupy our minds for the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">implementation of shariah in our daily life. Success both in this as well as in the next world lies in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the strict adherence and compliance of the dictates of shariah.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Three</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">principal</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">elements</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">constitute shariah.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">These are Elm (knowledge), Amol (practice),</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and Ekhlas (sacred intention). These three elements are complementary to each other. Discarding</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">one of these, make the others superfluous and redundant. It is imperative therefore to try and gain</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">mastery over all these elements.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Let us now describe precisely all the components one by one.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Elm : Knowledge is the light while ignorance is darkness. The prime objective of knowledge is to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">eliminate darkness of falsehood and guide mankind to the enlightened path of truth. Education in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Islam is mandatory (farz). This education is to acquire the knowledge of religion and shariah. The</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">greatest prophet declared, “ Education is compulsory for each muslim man and woman” (Ebne</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Ma-aza).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Every Muslim man and woman are required to know the commands and sanctions of Allah.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Acquiring</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">knowledge</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">on halal (recommended), haram (prohibited), farz (mandatory)</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and wazeb (obligatory) are also necessary. Mention has been made in the Hadith (sayings and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">deeds of the prophet) that knowledge is of two kinds. They are exoteric (worldly) and esoteric</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">(spiritual) knowledge. The Holy prophet only mentioned knowledge as Farz. He did not specify</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">any particular type. It transpires therefore that since knowledge is of two types-both the types ate</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">compulsory to acquire. Before that we should have a clear idea on the line of demarcation as</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">regards a thing becoming farz.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We all are aware that Islam is founded on five principal articles of faith called five pillars. These</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">are kalema (admitting the sacred sentence : La ilaha illallahu Mohammadur Rasulullah), salat (five</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">times prayers), siam (fasting), Hajj (pilgrimage to Mecca), and Zakat (charity). To be able to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">emulate and practise the fundamental concept of Islam in our daily life it becomes imperative to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">learn and acquire clear and correct knowledge on these principal aspects of our religious. Let’s</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">elaborate it with an example. If a man has no means there is no binding on him to perform Hajj or</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">to give away Zakat.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">It is therefore, not farz for him to acquire knowledge on these subjects. As a matter of fact,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">acquiring knowledge becomes contingent on the applicability of the dictates of shariah on</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">individual requirements for practising the same in this lifetime.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Nor it is required for every muslim to memorise the Quran (Hafez), to become a Muhaddis (Hadith</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">specialist) or to be a commentator of the Quran (Mufassir). In the similar manner it is not Farz to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">acquire knowledge to become Kutub, Gaus, Abdal, Aotad or Mujadded (These are different</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">administrative positions in the supernatural world. Depth of wisdom makes eligible for different</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">posts).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Education is only compulsory to the extent required to drive away from heart all affinities and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">attractions for material objects other the love for Allah- till the soul (kalb) turns placid and serene.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah declares thus “None of your wealth or offspring will benefit you on the day of Resurrection</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">save a calm and serene soul ” (Sura Suarah).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The soul (kalb) attains this sublime state only when enlightened with the zikir (remembrance) of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah. Nothing else but constant zikir of Allah can and will achieve such peaceful and placid state.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah Almighty declares “ kalb is salvaged and emancipated only through zikir of Allah” (sura-</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Raad).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Exoteric knowledge is acquired by enrolling in a religious school and studying therein. A teacher</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">will teach a student who has no knowledge of alphabets. In similar manner, a man aspiring for</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">spiritual</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">knowledge</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">needs</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">approach</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">a</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">man</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">whose kalb burns</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">with</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">fire</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of zikir (remembrance) of Allah. To derive maximum benefit from his association he must</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">completely submit himself to his morshed. The incessant cosmic ray that falls on the kalb of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the morshed (peer) is directed on the kalb of the disciple (murid) through deep and passionate</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">attachment and unstinted love and adulation and ignite the kalb of the disciple with the zikir of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah. The kalb only then turns sublime and emancipated. A man specialised in the art of such</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">divine knowledge is called a peer (spiritual guide). One should devote oneself in the pursuit of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">spiritual knowledge concurrently with general education. Otherwise he will be responsible for</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">non-compliance of his farz.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Amol : One should practise (amol) proportionate to his learning. The objective of learning is to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">practise. Without amol learning becomes meaningless unless applied, exercised and worked-out.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We are slaves of Allah. Our primary duty is to fulfil the demands of and meet obligations of a slave.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We should acquire knowledge in pursuits of this divine obligation and objective. Practise as we</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">learn and earn should be our prime motto.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Ekhlas : Ekhlas means acquiring unalloyed intention or honest motive behind any action</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">(niyat). Niyat is nothing but pure determination or fair resolve. Intention or a firm resolve should</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">be fair, honest and holy. For whatever degree of knowledge is acquired and amol done it is worth</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">nothing unless intended for the sole purpose of carrying our the orders and achieving complete</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">satisfaction and pleasure to Allah. Ekhlas being of such fundamental importance to our religious</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">concept, we shall discuss briefly the ways and means to achieve sacred intention (niyat).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The seat of origin of any sacred design (niyat) is soul. Expressing intention aloud is not mandatory.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">But to resolve in our hearts is definitely an act of farz. It is necessary to intend any resolve in our</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">hearts before performing salat. Further it is mentioned in the Hadith that “Amol (act of practice)</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">is judged by its intention ”(Bokhari).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Kalb is the seat of all activities both good and evil. The Satan (devil) has an abode there.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The Satan our enemy provokes us to commit misdeeds from there. No sacred or honest intention</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">can be conceived so long Satan sits in and exercises control over our kalb. The kalb is tarnished</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">with the presence of Satan occupying it. As a sequel, the niyat (intention) of an impure soul is</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">bound to be other than sacred and holy. The Satan has to be driven off from our heart in order to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">sanctify our soul. It will only then be possible to attain holy intention. It has come in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the Hadith that, The seat of Satan is deep rooted in our heart. The Satan runs away from the soul</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">with the zikir of Allah. But as soon as our kalb is negligent or oblivious of zikir, Satan makes a come</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">back and provokes us to commit evils or sins (Bokhari).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The Satan is most active during prayer and vehemently resists and diverts our mind at the time of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">performing salat. He (Satan) is conscious of the fact that he can not counter the strength bestowed</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">on us by Allah and developed through devoted prayers.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Salat (prayer) is the essence of all worships. When performed with devotion, it abstains us from all</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">evils and wrong doings. Almighty Allah declared, “ Surely salat refrains men from all wrong</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">doings” (Sura-Ankabut). Salat performed with ablution, clean dress and calm mind solely for the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">entire satisfaction of Allah maintaining correct sequence of all rituals considered perfect and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">accepted by Him.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah is most hallowed and sacred. No one but a most pious man is able to worship Him befittingly</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and successfully. Purity or sanctity both physical and mental are essential pre-requisites. Ablution</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">or bathing makes a man externaly clean but zikir of Allah causes to turn the kalb submissive and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">sublime.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">It is expedient for the kalb to be absorbed in zikir of Allah and continue always to do so.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">If kalb abstains from the zikir, Satan takes over the control of our kalb and destroys all good</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">intentions</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of amols and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">also</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">ruins</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">concentration</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of salat (prayer).</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">It</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">has</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">come</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the Hadith that “Salat performed without concentration of mind is futile.” Almighty Allah declares</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">“ Establish salat for the sake of my remembrance.” (Sura Ta-ha)</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We must find a way out of this precarious situation. We shall have to approach a spiritual guide</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">(peer) to realise that goal. We should search for a peer who is able to kindle our kalb(heart) with</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the ceaseless zikir of Allah. A genuine peer specialises in the art of such knowledge.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Understandably the clear objective of adopting to the prevalent schools of Sufism is to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">acquire ekhlas. Most of our people flock around a peer to fulfil material gains. Many of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">these peers also indulge in and are found to encourage such perverted aims and desires of these</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">people which by any standard is outside the purview of any genuine mystic school of thoughts.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">In</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">light</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">our</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">short</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">discussion</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">brief</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">deliberation,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">it</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">is</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">now</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">crystal</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">clear</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">that Elm, amol and ekhlas,- all these three constitute essential elements of true faith. Without one,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the other two are valueless. As for instance, a man having only elm means nothing without having</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">achieved amol (practice) and ekhlas. In similar way a person with elm and ekhlas but having</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">no amol to his credit it worth nothing.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">It is therefore understood that one has to be a follower and member of one of the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">different tarikas to be able to practise shariah fully. Based on the consideration of these facts and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">views tarika is regarded as a step forward towards shariah.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Now there may definitely arise some queries regarding the tarika to be adopted and practised. The</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">prime aim of all tarikas is one and identical. But Tarika-e-khas Mojaddedia has tremendous</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">advantage over others in respect of suitability and appropriateness and suits to the modern age.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Unlike other Tarika it is not necessary to work very hard to achieve spiritual development in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">this tarika. Allah likes both hard as well as easy religious exercises (amol). Life now is very</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">complicated and complex. Time too is very short. In view of all these it is imperative to adopt and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">practise a tarika as simple as possible.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">In Tarika-e-khas mojaddedia (a school of mystical thought propounded by Hazrat Mojaddede Alfe</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">sani (rh.), a murid (disciple) attains with ease complete spiritual development and progress</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">as noor (beam of concentrated light) is reflected from the kalb of peer to that of murid and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">illuminates the innerself of the later. This process of propagating and transmitting noor from</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the kalb of peer to the kalb of murid is achieved instantly i,e as soon as a disciple surrenders and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">submits himself fully to the will and discretion of the Peer and maximum time required may be</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">forty days. The next stages and phases of advancement in this spiritual process that follow are also</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">very rapid and accomplished very easily and quickly.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">It should however be borne in mind that tarika itself does not carry anyone to destination. Tarika is</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">a vehicle and peer is its guide. The fact is that the peer carries one to destination not the carrier</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">itself. However majestic and grand a tarika may be only accomplished peer can steer his disciples</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">to ultimate goal through his sheer spiritual potentials.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Nevertheless, in the absence of above stated qualifications or abilities or the peer, mere</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">surrendering to him will fail to produce the desired objectives.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We are muslims. Let us recall the verses of Allah “ Kullu muslimun Ekhwatun ” “All muslims are</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">a brotherhood.” We shall not forget another Hadith “Ummatun Oahedatun ”. “My followers are an</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">unity.” Muslims now-a-days are in great distress and calamity. We are subjects of hatred, neglect</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and oppression all over the world. Notwithstanding the fact that we should have been the leader</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of all races and nations like our prophet Mohammad ﷺ who was the leader of all prophets.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">But what do we find in reality ? We are subjected to the worst oppression and humiliation all the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">world over whether in a muslim majority or minority country. In some places our worldly passions</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and greeds ate set ablaze by the affluence of newly found wealth. Whereas in most of other places</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the reverse is true i.e. repression, oppression, illiteracy all as a by-product of proverty or being a</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">minority have become a stark reality.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">We shall have to find a way out of this morbid state quickly and immediately. Let us all plunge</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">ourselves in a holy war to recover our lost glory and usurped splendour. Self analysis to free</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">ourselves from all our shortcomings are also necessary. We should remember whether we are a</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">farmer, a labour or a head of state that we are slaves of Almighty Allah and followers of prophet</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Mohammad ﷺ. The duty of our head of state is to introduce Islamic rules in our country to</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">ensure justice in the society. Let us not forget that the only perfect law is the law of Almighty</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Allah . Oh, head of states! How dare you practise law that opposes the faith in Allah. Remember</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">you are sure will be accountable and held answerable by Almighty Allah in the day of Final</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Judgement.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The responsibility vested with the religious scholars is to imbue the general masses in the pursuits</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of religious education, to answer and settle problems of the contemporary world in the light of the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Holy Quran and the Hadith in addition to revival and enhancement of the glorious tradition, spirit</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and heritage in the exercise and practise of shariah. They consider to be the heir to the noble</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">prophet; but do they have the required quality, spirit and sacrifices? Oh, Scholars (alem)! Do you</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">think that you are doing the duty of a representative of the prophet? Are you not competing with</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">each other for name, fame, honour, wealth or power? Are you oblivious of the sayings of the noble</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">prophet that “That scholar will suffer most in the day of judgement who fails to derive benefit out</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of his erudition and learning.”</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">The aims of the sufi community is to illuminate the kalb of men with the light of love for Allah. But</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">what is the actual state of affairs? Oh, sufis! you are engaged in amassing wealth and other material</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">acquisitions and sycophancy of the influentials has become your principal aim of life. Why do you</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">offer and introduce yourselves as servants of religion but found engaged in opposing the faith itself</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">overtly or covertly?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">There are three categories of enemies of Islam. For instance, greedy rulers, selfish scholars and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">false and fake Sufis. Unless these three kinds of people are set on the right path, the glory and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">splendour of our faith will not shine and glow. Pious heads of states, religious scholars and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">accomplished Sufis can only contribute to the flourishing and glorifying our religion.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Common muslims are not directly involved in sacrilegious acts. Even then they cannot evade and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">shun the responsibility of distorting faith. It is incumbent upon them to find religious scholars and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">approach proficient spiritual guide in search of theological knowledge. Instead, they approach</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">them for fulfilling their material objectives.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">A great majority of these people are in fact trading in the name of religion to flourish self interest</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and to further self advancement.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">On the otherhand, their exists a current of thought opposed to everything in Islam. Islam is</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">anathema or least an conundrum to these so called intellectuals. Most of such pseudo</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">intellectuals are submerged in the cross current of irreligious and unIslamic pursuits. They too</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">cannot shun their moral obligations. While they spend day and night studying, reading and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">researching on every thing prejudicial to or repugnant to the tenets of Islam, they seem most</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">reluctant and unwilling to spend a fraction of their time, energy and resources in the study and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">research of the Holy Quran. Are they not aware that the knowledge of faith and dictates</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">of Sharia are Farz for</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">the</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">literate,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">illiterate,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">labours,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">farmers,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">journalists,</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">scientists</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">intellectuals all alike?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Our head of states, religious scholars, spiritual leaders and socially conscious educated section- all</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">are muslims and part of the same community. We cannot remain silent spectators or indifferent</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">to any of them going astray. We are all but an unique entity. “ ummatun Oahedatun” (my followers</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">are an unity). We are conscious of the fact that loss of any or damage to any of our single limb</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">weakens our strength. So let us then contemplate and reflect in collective self criticism and ponder</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">in retrospect :</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">1. Whether our creeds (Akida) are correct and flawless ?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">2. Do we consider both the currents of thinking (religious education and spiritual development)</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">equally important or not ?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">3. Whether we are practising (Amol) according to our learning (Elm) ?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">4. Whether we have enrolled in any school of spiritual knowledge (tarika) suitable for the modern</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">time ?</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Our own life should be adorned and equipped with the realistic answers to the questions raised</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">above. We should then propagate the theme and motivate others and sway the general masses in</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">all spheres of our family,. Social, national and international arena. Success in establishing religious</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">faith collectively will only then be a reality. The demand of tablig (propagation), ekamat-e-</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">deen and that of waging a holy war ( jehad) can then be realised.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">A man without adorning deen (Faith) in personal level cannot establish the same in social and</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">national life. It will indeed be an impossible proposition.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Oh, Brethren in Islam! Be aware! There is still time to involve ourselves to the total service to our</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">religion with the sole purpose of establishing and practising divine faith in every sphere of our</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">social structure and national life.</p>
<p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed mb-4 font-inter">Oassalamu Awalan wa Akheran.</p>
