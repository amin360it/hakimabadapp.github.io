Welcome

Hakimabad

Khanka-e-Mozaddedia — a spiritual sanctuary following the Tarika-e-Khas Mojaddedia, an unbroken chain of illumination tracing back to Prophet Muhammad (SAW).

Hakimabad is dedicated to spreading Islamic knowledge and guiding seekers to the path of Allah through proper understanding, sincere practice, and spiritual purification.
