About

Welcome to Hakimabad

Khanka-e-Mozaddedia — A spiritual sanctuary following the path of the righteous predecessors

Tarika-e-Khas Mojaddedia

A school of mystical thought propounded by Hazrat Mojaddede Alfe Sani (rh.). This spiritual path has been transmitted through an unbroken chain of 35+ saints from Prophet Muhammad (SAW) to the present day.

Hakimabad is dedicated to spreading the light of Islamic knowledge and helping seekers find the path to Allah through proper understanding, sincere practice, and spiritual purification.

Three Elements of Faith

علم
Elm

Knowledge is light — Seek knowledge from cradle to grave

عمل
Amol

Practice — Act upon your knowledge with devotion

اخلاص
Ekhlas

Sincerity — Purify your intention for Allah alone
