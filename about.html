About Hakimabad

Hakimabad is a spiritual center dedicated to Islamic learning and guidance, following the Tarika-e-Khas Mojaddedia - a school of mystical thought that traces its lineage back to Prophet Muhammad (SAW) through a chain of 35+ saints.

The center provides Islamic education, organizes regular mahfils (religious gatherings), and disseminates Islamic knowledge through audio recordings, videos, and publications.

Our mission is to help seekers find the path to Allah through proper understanding and practice of Islam, following the sunnah of the prophet and the guidance of our spiritual predecessors.

Khanka-e-Mozaddedia

The Khanka (Spiritual Lodge) follows the Tarika-e-Khas Mojaddedia, which combines the teachings of multiple Sufi orders: Qadria, Chistia, Naqshbandia, and Mujaddedia.

This unique synthesis was propagated by Hazrat Mujaddid-e-Alfe Sani (Imam Ahmad Faruqi Serhindi) and continues through our present spiritual lineage.

Mohammad Mamunur Rashid � Biography Mohammad Mamunur Rashid was the founder and spiritual leader of Hakimabad. He carried the ijazah (authorization) in multiple Sufi orders, including Qadria, Chistia, Naqshbandia, and Mujaddedia. He devoted his life to spreading Islamic knowledge and guiding seekers along the spiritual path. Early Life and Calling Born into a family rooted in religious learning, Mohammad Mamunur Rashid felt an early calling to spiritual study and service. He pursued traditional Islamic education and spiritual training, receiving ijazah from recognized masters in several Sufi lineages. These authorizations shaped his approach to teaching and pastoral care. Spiritual Leadership and Mission As the spiritual head of Hakimabad, he emphasized practical spirituality, ethical conduct, and heartfelt devotion. His mission was to make authentic Islamic teachings accessible to people from diverse backgrounds and to help aspirants progress steadily on the path of inner transformation. He traveled, taught, and established gatherings where seekers could learn, practice, and receive guidance. Migration and Global Outreach He later immigrated to Cambodia, from where he continued his outreach and teaching. Through lectures, mahfils, and community programs, he worked to build spiritual networks and support systems for students both locally and abroad. Teachings and Media His lectures focused on Quranic exegesis, prophetic guidance, spiritual discipline, and practical advice for daily life. Audio recordings and videos of his mahfils and talks were made available to the public, enabling students worldwide to benefit from his guidance. He also contributed written works and translations that reflected his commitment to education and spiritual formation. Legacy Mohammad Mamunur Rashid left behind a living legacy of students, recorded teachings, and a spiritual center in Hakimabad. His emphasis on sincere practice, compassionate leadership, and the transmission of traditional Sufi knowledge continued to inspire seekers and scholars alike.
