Founder and Spiritual Leader — Hakimabad Khanka‑e‑Mozaddedia

Primary repository: Hakimabad official site; audio/video archives and publications indexed on site.

Executive Summary

Mohammad Mamunur Rashid was the founder and spiritual leader of Hakimabad Khanka‑e‑Mozaddedia. He carried formal ijazah (authorizations) in multiple Sufi orders — Qadria, Chistia, Naqshbandia, and Mujaddedia — and devoted his life to teaching, spiritual formation, and community service. His recorded lectures, mahfil audio, and written works are preserved and distributed through the Hakimabad website and affiliated media channels.

Long Formal Biography

Early Life and Formation

Born into a family committed to traditional Islamic learning, Mohammad Mamunur Rashid pursued classical religious education from an early age. He combined textual scholarship with the practical disciplines of Sufi training, entering into discipleship under established masters and receiving formal ijazah that authorized him to teach and guide others.

Spiritual Lineage and Authorizations

Rashid’s spiritual credentials included authorizations in the Qadria, Chistia, Naqshbandia, and Mujaddedia tariqas. His teaching emphasized transmission through recognized chains of instruction, adherence to the Quran and Sunnah, and the ethical and devotional practices central to classical Sufism.

Mission and Teaching Emphases

His mission centered on spreading authentic Islamic knowledge and guiding seekers along the spiritual path. Core emphases included:

  • Quranic exegesis and practical tafsir for daily life
  • Prophetic guidance (Hadith) and moral formation
  • Sufi disciplines: dhikr, muraqabah, and ethical training
  • Accessible instruction for lay audiences and structured study for advanced students

Migration and International Outreach

He extended his outreach internationally and continued teaching after relocating abroad. Hakimabad’s event listings and media archives document regular mahfils, seasonal gatherings, and remote instruction for students worldwide.

Legacy and Institutional Continuity

Mohammad Mamunur Rashid left a living institutional legacy in the form of Hakimabad Khanka‑e‑Mozaddedia, a network of students, recorded teachings, and published materials that continue to be used for instruction and spiritual formation.
